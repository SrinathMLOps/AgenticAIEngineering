WORLD'S 3 MOST POPULOUS COUNTRIES - POPULATION ANALYSIS
========================================================
World Population Reference: 8.1 billion (8,100,000,000)
Date: 2024-2025 estimates

RANKING BY POPULATION:

1. INDIA
   Population: 1,428,000,000 (1.428 billion)
   Percentage of World Population: 17.63%
   Status: Most populous country since 2023

2. CHINA  
   Population: 1,404,000,000 (1.404 billion)
   Percentage of World Population: 17.33%
   Status: Second most populous, experiencing population decline

3. UNITED STATES
   Population: 341,784,857 (341.8 million)
   Percentage of World Population: 4.22%
   Status: Third most populous country

SUMMARY:
- India and China together represent 34.96% of world population
- All three countries combined represent 39.18% of world population
- India surpassed China as the most populous country in 2023
- China's population is declining while India's continues to grow slowly
- The US has significantly fewer people than the Asian giants but remains the most populous Western nation

Data Sources: Wikipedia Demographics pages, UN estimates, US Census Bureau
